About Road Renegade

Road Renegade is a free geography puzzle game where players must escape from one city and reach another while avoiding wanted states and completing required detours.

The game was created as an independent coding project and combines geography, route planning, and puzzle-solving into a replayable challenge.

The goal is simple: reach your destination while using as few cities as possible and overcoming the restrictions of each challenge.
